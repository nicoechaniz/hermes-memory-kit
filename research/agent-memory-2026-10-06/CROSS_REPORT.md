## A. Conclusions

1. ❓ Improve HMK's preservation and native write/recall contract before replacing
   its canon. Its confirmed losses are implementation defects; adopting a graph
   or a more capable model does not repair missing capture or history. [R1][R2][R5]
2. ✅ Separate episodes/evidence, evolving accounts and inferred understanding.
   Hindsight, Honcho and newer provenance research offer concrete patterns.
   ❓ Preserve selected sufficient episodes even when current syntheses change. [R2][R3]
3. ✅ Dreaming exists in Anthropic's current research preview and other systems.
   📊 Consolidation can also reduce benchmark accuracy. ❓ Treat it as a staged,
   measured transformation with recoverable inputs and explicit effects. [R1]
4. ❓ Capture meaningful authorized inputs before their source disappears, then
   consolidate them asynchronously or in finite foreground work. Tool responses,
   ordinary shared moments and brief encounters must be eligible. [R1][R3]
5. ✅ Modern evaluations test updates, temporal reasoning, implicit cues,
   obsolete memory and learned environment experience. ❓ None establishes our
   ten-year, source-loss, cross-body continuity requirement by itself. [R4]
6. ❓ Prepare versioned export and shadow comparisons now; replace an index,
   extractor or backend only when measured requirements justify it and restore,
   provenance and receiving acceptance pass. [R5]

## C. Proposed native record contract

- ❓ A selected episode carries participants and known identifiers, source
  event/version, originating body and narrator, what happened, why it mattered,
  the being's involvement, observed follow-up and remaining uncertainty. Its
  meaning survives unavailable source sessions and media. [R1][R3]
- ❓ Memory kind (episode, knowledge, procedure) and mode of knowing (observed,
  reported, inferred, generated) are separate axes. Local IDs/name similarity
  do not establish portable actor identity or authenticated membership. [R2][R3]
- ❓ Occurrence may be an interval, approximate or unknown. Keep report/mention,
  recording and revision times separately; no unknown date becomes today. [R2][R3]
- ❓ Current accounts have stable logical identity and expected-version writes.
  Preserve old native versions and episodes atomically; distinguish changed true
  claims from corrected mistakes and unresolved disagreement. [R2][R3][R5]
- ❓ Protected Matrix/Wiki material keeps its source authority; native revisions
  do not become competing authoritative events. Preserve origin/classification
  through compact recall as well as expansion. [R2][R3][R5]
- ❓ Text preserves meaningful media content; authorized assets retain caption,
  hash, source and mode. Generated illustrations are not scene evidence. [R3]

## D. Capture and dream lifecycle

- ✅ The proposed upstream HMK distiller excludes tool messages, truncates
  sessions and has no durable source checkpoint. LangMem's local debounce can
  replace pending work; its producer must retain undigested inputs. [R1]
- ❓ Use a per-body/per-stream ordered manifest and stable source event/version.
  A source correction is new input. Policy/model versions identify processing
  decisions; a policy rerun does not create a second lived encounter. [R1][R5]
- ❓ Persist enough selected pending evidence for retry; acknowledge applied or
  deliberately omitted decisions only after durable outcomes. Failed/deferred
  gaps remain discoverable, with no silent tail truncation or fixed fact quota. [R1]
- ❓ Prompt capture protects irreplaceable episodes; a later dream updates linked
  accounts, proposes patterns and skill refinements. Validate proposals and keep
  source evidence/history. A dreamed hypothesis remains a hypothesis. [R1][R3]
- ❓ Distinguish committed/indexed text from embedding-ready text. Retry missing
  vectors and rendering separately; a diary failure cannot undo memory. [R1][R5]
- ❓ Foreground invocation is the initial integration. Future daily, idle,
  step-count, pre-compression or end triggers use the same contract under each
  body's authorization. No scheduler, hooks or attention were installed here. [R1]

## E. Retrieval, diary and procedural continuity

- ❓ Repair Unicode/full-question cues, identity-preserving deduplication,
  corpus-specific priors, incoming/outgoing links and the relevance gate before
  quotas/fusion. Preserve lexical degraded results during vector outages. [R2]
- ❓ Query current accounts and historical episodes appropriately. Time filters
  require grounded bounds; storage recency must not erase old weak-cue recall. [R2][R4]
- ❓ Person/project accounts supply multiple retrieval entry points. Project
  capsules remember authored contributions and dated status; repositories retain
  detailed operational state and fresh verification. [R2][R3]
- ❓ Keep lessons linked to experience and tested procedures in maintained
  packages. The being can remember an affair or another body's skill without
  this body claiming its tools or capabilities. [R1][R3]
- ❓ Render a diary from canonical episodes and selected assets. It is a
  rebuildable navigation surface, with corrections and missing media visible. [R1][R3]
