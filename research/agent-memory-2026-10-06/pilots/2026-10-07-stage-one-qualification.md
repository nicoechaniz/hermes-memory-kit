# Stage 1 qualification — 2026-10-07

Stage 1's exit criteria pass, with exact attributed support as the qualified
answer form. This report separates selected memory, retrieval, supported answers and cost.
It qualifies the finite fictional capture/recall procedure, not ten real years
of deployment, unattended coverage, fluent narrative reliability, consolidation
or transport to another live harness. The owning work is
[HMK #13](https://github.com/nicoechaniz/hermes-memory-kit/issues/13) and the
[maintained roadmap](../../../docs/durable-recall-plan.md).

## Procedure and fair comparison

The main corpus contains 12 fictional input cases, 14 distinct retained meanings,
48 retention essentials across 15 account/event expectations, and 16 questions
with 37 answer requirements. A supplemental corpus contains an independent
failed chart handover and 100 repeated transport lines: two cases/questions,
one retained meaning, three retention essentials and four answer requirements.
Expected meanings, questions and rubrics were absent from formation requests.
Recall requests contained the current question, fictional receiving binding and
bounded retrieved support, without rubrics, source sessions, URL access or tools
from the original bodies.

Both arms used the same current repaired runtime, source order, NVIDIA chat
model `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b` (temperature 0, low reasoning,
6,000 output-token ceiling), configured embedding model
`nvidia/nemotron-3-embed-1b`, general retrieval profile and disabled reranking.
Each primary/follow-up pack had threshold 0.4, a 1,500 estimated-token budget
and five primary candidates. At most two follow-up queries and five visible
primary/one-hop expansion IDs were allowed. Guidance differed between the
pinned original librarian and the proposed durable-selection reference.
This compares policies under the repaired procedure; it cannot isolate the
effect of each runtime repair or establish superiority over another product.

Formation selected meaningful source blocks rather than generating factual
paraphrases. The adapter preserved exact attributed text, source/channel,
report date and originating body through supported native writers. Arbitrary
source IDs, generated raw/summary and recognition titles outside selected text
were refused. Native UUIDs/revisions and their pre-images remained available.
The completed formation was reused unchanged in separate receiving snapshots;
reuse is not another independent capture trial. Its original costs are attributed
once and separately from each later receiving experiment.

The receiving database excluded `capture_events` and `capture_streams`; selected
text remained available as memory, without the original unselected input ledger.
The retrieval clock advanced 315,576,000 seconds, while selected creation/update
timestamps remained unchanged. Three unrelated, recently dated SandNet relay
maintenance records were injected after formation solely as retrieval distractors.
They are excluded from selected-memory and noise-admission counts. The receiving
voice body had no network, repository/runtime, physical sensor/actuator or private
session access; code/mobile history belonged to the same fictional being, while
Reed remained a distinct being.

## Why exact supported answers

The [repair record](2026-10-07-stage-one-repairs.md) preserves the failures that
motivated each change. The original partial pilot had a contaminated guide
example, weak-cue retrieval failure, omitted meaningful failed action and invalid
plan interruption. Later procedures retained the invitation but invented
simulation or participation in captured prose. A second model review still
turned a human report into voice-body autobiography. Source-block capture fixed
that formation risk; free-form recall then reintroduced unsupported simulation,
human/body attribution and definite non-receipt from merely unobserved access.

The qualified answer contract therefore selects evidence IDs for identification,
context, meaning, outcome and limits. The adapter returns the exact supplied
blocks and their attribution, without generating factual narration. Empty facets
mean unsupported in the supplied context, not proof that an event never happened.
The receiving binding is returned separately, and dated project evidence is
explicitly last-known state. Prose, unseen IDs, wrong receiver identity and
inconsistent facet/citation unions are refused. This is a narrower, verifiable
recall result; narrative fidelity remains unqualified. It must not be advertised
as a successful free-form answer benchmark by counting copied evidence as prose.

No benchmark-specific answer facts or expected IDs were installed in the
procedure. Formation still omitted routine sources. Evidence selection can
include redundant or loosely relevant retained support; that cost is reported
instead of hidden by manually editing the selected canon.

## Cost and compression

The manifest records model attempt counts, known provider prompt/completion/total
tokens and summed request latency per formation and receiving phase. It also
records raw/preview bytes, chapter-token estimates, native pre-image storage,
allocated receiving SQLite bytes, estimated pack tokens, expansion characters
and approximate expanded-JSON tokens. Database allocation includes indexes,
embeddings, native history and injected distractors, so it is not pure selected
text size. Estimated pack/expansion tokens are distinct from provider tokens.

Embedding traces record indexing/query attempts, text counts/characters, latency
and readiness/errors; their token usage and monetary billing are unavailable.
Unknown usage remains null, including interrupted/failed requests. The separate
[procedure-cost ledger](stage-one-evidence/procedure-cost-ledger.json) preserves available request logs and costs for
earlier rejected procedures as well; those costs are not silently removed from
the research effort or added twice as inherited formation.

the three main negative checks add 6,537/8,023; the supplemental noise rerun adds

Exact blocks provide trustworthy support but are not optimal organization:
the proposed formation duplicates the issue block in episode/entity accounts
and nests old account blocks in current project text while also retaining native
pre-images. Some responses select extra support. Stage 2 must improve useful
compression and navigation while preserving every essential and its history.
The smaller baseline record count alone does not establish better memory.
