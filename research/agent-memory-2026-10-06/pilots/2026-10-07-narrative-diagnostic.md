questions in v7 under identical receiving procedures. This is a complete
diagnostic run, not a completed final-contract qualification or a complete
independent assertion/requirement grading. It provides concrete counterexamples

## Corrections and exact continuation

The next procedure validates decoded pointers, equivalent full calendar dates,
unknown channel/body attribution, assertion-level source quotation membership,
exhaustive sentence decomposition and frozen actual answer context. Ordinary
retrieval now exposes transitive source staleness using the same dependency
checker as consolidation. These mechanical corrections have focused tests;
they do not establish narrative quality.

Next run a fresh complete comparison under the final receiving contract over
identical preserved fictional formation, explicitly recording the upgraded
reader and output budget. Independently grade all assertions and all 51 answer
requirements, plus selection, 60 essentials, preservation and costs. Do not
combine partial fixes into a fictitious complete success. Only after natural
narration qualifies execute the three organization passes, replay and dependent
correction/reconciliation, with recall after every transformation. Matrix
portability and native Codex coexistence remain later stages. No native memory,
hooks, timers or autonomous attention were activated.
