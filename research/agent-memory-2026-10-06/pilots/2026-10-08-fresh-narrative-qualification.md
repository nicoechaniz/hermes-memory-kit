## Selected roles and actual consumption

Keep NVIDIA `nvidia/nemotron-3-embed-1b` and its existing document/query space;
no reranker is introduced. Direct DeepSeek `deepseek-flash` low handles selection,
selection review, retrieval planning and initial narration. Native OpenAI Codex
`gpt-6.1-sol` low handles narrative review and bounded corrections. This is a
qualified mixed configuration over this corpus, not proof that one model or
provider is universally better. Earlier identical-packet Flash/Sol comparisons
remain separately linked in the roadmap; future Sol uses OpenAI Codex, not Nous.
Qwen/Gemini remain deferred.

[Costs](receiving-api-evidence/fresh-flash-pipeline-v3/costs.json) separate 238 new
physical receiving calls, all complete, totaling 2,827,093 known tokens. Median
completed call time is 21.90 seconds. Flash initial generation uses 401,506 tokens,
planning 104,408; native review uses 1,434,177, correction 861,700 and two structural
repairs 25,302. Original formation's 72 calls/528,169 tokens are reused provenance,
not new formation dispatches. There are 131 new retrieval embedding attempts;
original formation has five batches. Embedding token usage, actual dollar
billing, native subscription conversion and parent outside-inspection cost are
not measured as known charges.

Decoded-envelope character preflight was smaller, but the completed V3 procedure
has essentially the same known token total as V2. Different plans, prose and
repairs prevent treating this as an isolated encoding-cost experiment. Do not
claim a total-cost saving or general production latency from that preflight.
The extensive verification workflow costs substantially more than a first
narration. This finite experiment installs no live narrator or autonomous cycle.
