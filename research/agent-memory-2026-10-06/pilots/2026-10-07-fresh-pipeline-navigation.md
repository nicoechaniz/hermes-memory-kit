# Fresh Flash/native-Sol pipeline: navigation and handoff failure

This complete-formation attempt does **not** qualify stage two. Both arms
selected all 60 essential meanings and excluded mechanical noise, but selection
and an accepted narrative do not establish usable recall.

The trial uses direct DeepSeek Flash low for selection, selection review,
retrieval planning and initial narration; OpenAI Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` low supplies
narrative review and bounded correction. NVIDIA embeddings are unchanged.
The eighteen fictional cases, hidden requirements, source loss, actual ten-year
retrieval-clock advance and recent distractors are identical across the arms.
No live memory, tools, native memory or private sessions enter receiving prompts.
This is a full pipeline/configuration trial, not a model-only comparison.

## Observed result

[Outside assessment](receiving-api-evidence/fresh-flash-pipeline-v1/outside-assessment.json)
inspects all 551 final candidate claims and all 102 positive requirements.
The proposed arm saves 22 accepted candidates, but covers only 48/51 positive
requirements: it reports no stored acknowledgment lesson even though selection
retained that lesson. One separate claim adds an occurrence year to a June 8
encounter using only a fully dated receipt. Automatic review accepted both errors.

The control saves 21 candidates, with two semantic rejections. Its last question
ends in structural rejection after a single repair: the actual candidate gives
a bounded unknown and older context but labels the older method as a facet of
the missing encounter. Its four actual sentences remain in pending evidence;
there is no fabricated empty fallback. All 51 control requirements are present
in actual candidates, but only 44 are delivered by accepted answers. The runner
exits with an error, so no complete-run success or stage-two gate is claimed.

Generic verbal explanation is distinct from original physical tools. The
control's method/ability basis mixtures cause protocol rejection; they are not
invented physical attendance. Source/report dates, uncertain past attribution,
shared personal participation and explicit absence of additional Neri identifiers
were inspected against the literal sources.

## Demonstrated cause and correction

Navigation limited **edges**, so multiple relations to the same chapter consumed
multiple slots. For the proposed project, compact hints were `[1, 3, 3]`.
Deduplicating targets produces `[1, 3, 5]`; it alone still does not deliver lesson
10 within three hints. Full expansion already supplies lesson 10, but the pilot
handoff discarded expanded-neighbor previews. The accepted narrator therefore
never received the lesson. This is a retrieval/handoff failure, not evidence that
a stronger narrator could reconstruct missing text.

HMK now bounds distinct linked chapters, reads each once and retains all relation
metadata in full expansion. Compact hints retain the primary relation without
duplicating targets. The pilot passes already returned attributed neighbor
previews to the narrator; complete expanded records take precedence. This adds
neither new selected noise nor sources outside the returned evidence.

Complete diagnostics also archive terminal structural failures without resetting
budgets or discarding the actual candidate, and observe both worker outcomes.
Resumable context/provider errors still propagate. Narrator/reviewer guidance
explicitly preserves month/day occurrence precision without borrowing a receipt
year; older context after a bounded unknown uses context/limits facets. The
qualification requirements remain unchanged.

Validation: 320 tests pass, including distinct-neighbor saturation, expanded
learning handoff/full-source precedence, and continuation after an archived
terminal malformed candidate. A new two-arm retrieval/narration trial is required
and running with the exact prior formation, an explicit reader upgrade and fresh
receiving conditions. The original failed attempt is preserved.
