# Source referents and URL punctuation: bounded receiving correction

The post-consolidation reader accepted two kinds of ownership drift: resolving
an indirect human report's unspecified “we” as the reporter and this being,
and turning a reported group's intention into our shared intention. Both the
original accepted candidates and their native reviews remain in the audit.
The stage queues were stopped; no successful stage-two result is inferred.

The experimental reader now optionally adds candidate-independent source
referents to decoded **Human report** blocks. Original quotations, headers,
identifiers and proof passages are unchanged. Singular first person remains the
reporter; plural membership requires event-specific evidence. Being addressed
by a wish does not establish participation in a different reported action,
intention or non-action. Direct conversations and explicit human-reported
participation remain usable. This is a source-format adapter, not an inferred
participant roster or a universal natural-language parser.

A separate deterministic defect treated prose's unmatched closing parenthesis
as part of an issue URL. A faithful answer then lacked that nonexistent pointer
and received another revision. Pointer extraction now removes only unmatched
closing delimiters and ordinary trailing punctuation, preserving balanced
characters within real URLs. The source-anchor procedure fingerprint changes;
historical checkpoints require their frozen implementation, not silent adoption.
The source-referents option also changes the procedure fingerprint.
