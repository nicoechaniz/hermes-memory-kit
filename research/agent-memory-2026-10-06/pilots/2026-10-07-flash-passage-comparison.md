# Complete Flash low passage-review comparison

The two frozen policy arms completed all 44 questions with DeepSeek Flash low
for narration, review and revisions. This trial **fails qualification**. Better
narration alone did not make the complete procedure reliable.

The [outside assessment](receiving-api-evidence/flash-reviewed-v6/outside-assessment.json)
records every final claim and every hidden positive/forbidden requirement;
[actual requests and receipts](receiving-api-evidence/flash-reviewed-v6/manifest.json)
retain all generation, review, repair and revision attempts. Rejected candidates
are preserved for diagnosis and are not delivered answers. No rubric entered
formation, planning, narration, review or structural repairs.
