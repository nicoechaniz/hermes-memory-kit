# Preserve relevant meaning within the existing narrative budget

A first-pass cold receiver reaches its twenty-sentence limit with a faithful
timeline. Native review asks it to restore the later acknowledgment lesson,
but both bounded revisions retain an unrelated instruction to update memory
and fail to make room for that lesson. The terminal candidate is rejected.
Outside inspection finds all twenty factual claims supported and all three
question requirements present; rejection still prevents delivery. This is a
single-question diagnostic, not successful full-pass qualification.

The [retained evidence](receiving-api-evidence/narrative-revision-headroom-v1)
includes the terminal checkpoint and eight actual planner/generation/review/
revision calls. Their known consumption is 134,679 tokens, median elapsed time
42.33 seconds per call; billing is unknown. The full V4 comparisons retain their
original procedure and continue separately. No rejected checkpoint is relabeled
as an accepted answer.

The final V5 guidance keeps twenty sentences, existing sentence length limits,
the same positive requirements and two semantic revisions. It asks a receiver
to preserve relevant meaning rather than every earlier sentence, removing
unrelated store/update instructions and redundant headers to restore relevant
learning without losing attribution or qualifications. Organization guidance
likewise keeps short dated illustrations and original pointers in procedural
understanding, leaving full chronology in preserved linked accounts/episodes.
It does not delete original detail or add receiving capabilities to memory.

This change is frozen before the fresh V5 comparison and snapshot recalls.
Existing narrative/organization checks pass (43 and two respectively); they do
not prove narrative quality. The new complete comparison and each cold snapshot
still require outside inspection of every claim and question requirement.

with matching request and role parameters, never an accepted answer, verdict or
revision budget; physical usage and reused responses must be reported separately.
