## The six things that matter

1. **Keep episodes, current accounts and learnings connected.** Updating what a
   project means now must preserve its encounters and contributions. A summary
   or source pointer alone cannot guarantee later recall after source loss.
   ❓ [R2][R3]
2. **Make capture reliable before making sleep clever.** Cover meaningful tools,
   actions, encounters and ordinary moments. Preserve pending selected evidence,
   use per-stream event/version checkpoints and commit outcomes before advancing.
   A daily pass or session-end callback alone cannot prevent loss. ❓ [R1][R3]
3. **Consolidate with staged, reversible changes.** Anthropic documents Dreams;
   other implementations offer related formation patterns. Published consolidation
   experiments also include regressions. Test preservation and recall after each
   pass; keep hypotheses distinct from autobiography. ✅ 📊 ❓ [R1]
4. **Upgrade HMK's native contract and recall.** Native revisions, source/body/mode,
   uncertain dates, actor identifiers, expected-version updates and maintained
   indexes accompany Unicode cues, bounded bidirectional navigation, provenance
   previews and explicit degraded/null retrieval. ❓ [R2][R3]
5. **Use a diary as a derived view.** Render meaningful episodes and selected
   media; retain textual memory when images disappear. Keep tested skills in
   maintained packages and remember which body can use them. ❓ [R1][R3]
6. **Prepare component replacement without committing to a wholesale swap.**
   No measured result here establishes that SQLite must go. Compare an index or
   extractor under the same continuity cases; require lossless available-corpus
   transfer, receiving acceptance and verified rollback for a canon change.
   ❓ [R4][R5]
